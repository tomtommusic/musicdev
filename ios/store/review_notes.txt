2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
MusiDev is a music education app designed by a high-school music teacher in Quebec, Canada. It is for music students (secondary school and up), hobby musicians and music teachers. It solves a common problem: students need regular ear-training and reading practice outside class, with instant feedback. MusiDev provides unlimited, randomly generated exercises (melodic and rhythmic dictation, intervals, chord progressions, note and rhythm reading, sight-reading, solfège) plus practice tools (tuner, metronome, recorder, chord dictionary, backing band) in one offline app, in French, English and Spanish.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No setup, login or sample files are needed.
- The home screen lists the modules (Modules button) and shows quick icons for the Metronome, Tuner, Recorder, Chord dictionary and Backing band.
- Exercises: open a module, press Play, answer on the staff or with the buttons, then Check.
- The tuner, the sight-reading/solfège pitch check and the recorder ask for microphone access on first use. Audio is processed on the device only; it is never stored on a server or sent anywhere.
- The "?" button (Explanations) opens the theory sheet for any element you tap.
- Language (FR/EN/ES) and light/dark mode are at the top of the home screen.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app runs entirely on the device and works offline. It uses no analytics, advertising, authentication, data providers, payment processors (other than the App Store purchase) or AI services. All sounds are generated or bundled in the app.

5. REGIONS
The app works identically in all regions. Only the interface language changes (French, English or Spanish).

Not applicable. MusiDev is not in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material: all exercises, texts and sounds are original or generated by the app.
